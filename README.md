# 🌾 AgroBuddy

### Mandi-to-Market Supply Chain Intelligence

> Turning messy agricultural data into actionable supply-chain insights.

AgroBuddy is our solution for **Track 3: AgriTech – Mandi-to-Market Supply Chain Optimizer** of the TransOrg AgentIQ Datathon.

The project combines mandi arrivals, market prices, MSP, weather conditions, transportation logistics, and mandi master data into a unified analytical system.

Our goal is not just to visualize agricultural data, but to identify **supply disruptions, price pressure, weather impact, and logistics bottlenecks** across the mandi-to-market supply chain.

---

# 1. Problem Statement

Agricultural supply chains generate data across multiple operational systems:

- Mandi arrivals
- Wholesale prices
- Minimum Support Price (MSP)
- Weather conditions
- Transportation
- Warehouses
- Mandi and district information

However, these sources often contain inconsistent identifiers, units, dates, currencies, and measurements.

This makes it difficult to answer operational questions such as:

- Which mandis are experiencing supply pressure?
- Which crops are trading below MSP?
- Which transportation routes have frequent delays?
- Is rainfall associated with a decline in arrivals?
- Which mandis contribute the highest crop volume?
- Where are the major supply-chain bottlenecks?

AgroBuddy addresses this problem through a reproducible **Data Rescue → Analytics → Intelligence → Visualization** pipeline.
